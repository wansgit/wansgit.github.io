About the Author

AP

Adi Pratama

Content Writer & Tech Enthusiast

I am a technology enthusiast who has been in the IT world since 2015. My passion for technical details led me to explore the ins and outs of file formats and file extensions that we use daily but rarely realize the importance of.

I started my career in IT support, where I often had to help colleagues who were confused about opening files with unfamiliar formats. That's when I realized that many people still lack understanding about file formats. This motivated me to create educational content about file extensions on FileExt.

Outside of writing, I also enjoy photography (which is why I understand RAW and HEIC files well), occasionally coding small projects, and of course exploring various new software. If you have any questions or suggestions, don't hesitate to reach out through the contact page.
